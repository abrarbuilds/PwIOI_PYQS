## Required metadata for each paper

- Branch
- Semester
- Subject name and code
- Exam type: CT-1, CT-2, Midsem, or Endsem
- Year
- Clear PDF filename
- Cloudinary PDF URL after upload

## PDF quality checks

- Pages are readable at normal zoom on a phone.
- Paper is complete and in the correct order.
- Filename identifies the subject, exam type, and year.
- The PDF opens and downloads from its final URL.
